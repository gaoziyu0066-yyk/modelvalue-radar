# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome people of every background and experience level. This includes first-time contributors and people communicating in a second language.

Be respectful, patient, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Credit others' work, respect privacy, and make room for different perspectives. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam and deliberate disruption are not.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to impact and repeated behavior; serious threats may result in immediate removal.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's profile contact, if provided, or GitHub's **Report abuse** tools. Do not post personal information or allegations in a public issue. The project does not yet operate a separate private moderation mailbox. Maintainers must treat reports with care, avoid conflicts of interest, and protect reporter privacy to the extent possible.

Everyone, including maintainers, is expected to follow this code.
